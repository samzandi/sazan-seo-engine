# Fixture: Commercial / Service

## Scenario

A real service business creates a landing page for a service it actually offers.

## Expected intent

Commercial investigation or transaction, depending on the supplied brief.

## Required checks

- Service scope, price claims, availability, guarantees, certifications and contact details must come from verified project context.
- The page must state the offer and conversion path clearly.
- Metadata must describe the real offer without clickbait or fabricated scarcity.
- Internal links should connect relevant service, trust and contact pages.
- Organization/Service structured data must match visible content.
- Primary and supporting keywords must be checked against the registry.

## Critical failure examples

- Invented discount, guarantee, certification or testimonial.
- Fake urgency.
- Schema containing information absent from or inconsistent with visible content.
- Intent collision with another canonical service page.
