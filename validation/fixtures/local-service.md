# Fixture: Local Service

## Scenario

A real business creates a location/service page for an area it genuinely serves.

## Expected intent

Local commercial/transactional.

## Required checks

- Business identity, address where applicable, phone, opening information and service area must be verified.
- Do not fabricate a physical office to rank in a target city.
- LocalBusiness subtype must match the actual business.
- NAP data must be internally consistent.
- Location copy must provide genuine local usefulness rather than city-name substitution.
- Canonical and indexability must be intentional.
- Similar location pages must be checked for duplication and doorway-page risk.

## Critical failure examples

- Fake address or branch.
- Unsupported service area.
- Boilerplate city-swapped doorway content.
- Structured data contradicting visible NAP information.
