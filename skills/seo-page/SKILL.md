# SEO Page

## Workflow
1. Establish page role, audience, target query, locale, and intended conversion.
2. Inspect title, meta description, canonical, robots directives, H1/H2/H3 structure, URL, copy, media, links, schema, and social metadata.
3. Verify intent match and content completeness before mechanical SEO checks.
4. Check entity clarity, factual support, author/business trust signals, freshness, and first-hand experience.
5. Review internal links in both directions and detect competing pages.
6. Review accessibility-relevant image alt text without keyword stuffing.
7. Review GEO/AEO readiness: direct answers, extractable facts, citations/sources, structured sections, and unambiguous entities.
8. Score each issue as Pass / Needs work / Fail and attach a specific fix.

## Output
Return prioritized findings grouped as Critical, High, Medium, Low, followed by a proposed corrected page plan and validation evidence.

## Guardrails
Do not treat arbitrary word counts or keyword-density percentages as ranking requirements. Do not add schema unsupported by visible/verified page facts.
