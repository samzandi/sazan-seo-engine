# Local SEO

## Inputs
Verified business name, address/service area, phone, hours, categories/services, target locations, website, and real proof/reviews when available.

## Workflow
1. Establish the canonical business identity and NAP source of truth.
2. Map services to legitimate service areas and local search intents.
3. Audit local landing pages for unique local value; reject doorway-page duplication.
4. Check LocalBusiness/Organization/Service schema against verified facts.
5. Review contact/location consistency, embedded directions/maps where useful, opening hours, service-area wording, and conversion paths.
6. Review review/testimonial usage without fabricating or rewriting claims as facts.
7. Identify citation/profile consistency opportunities.
8. Build local internal-link architecture among service, location, contact, and relevant informational pages.
9. Add locally useful evidence: regulations, neighborhoods, logistics, pricing factors, or service constraints only when verified.

## Guardrails
Never invent an address, review, rating, license, service area, office, or local experience. Do not generate near-duplicate city pages solely to capture keywords.
