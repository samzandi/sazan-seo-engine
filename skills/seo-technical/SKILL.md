# Technical SEO

## Audit dimensions
1. Crawlability and robots controls
2. Indexability and accidental noindex
3. Canonicalization and duplicate URLs
4. XML sitemap correctness
5. HTTP status, redirects, loops, and broken links
6. Rendering and discoverable HTML content
7. Mobile usability
8. Performance/Core Web Vitals risks
9. Structured data validity and factual consistency
10. URL architecture and internal crawl paths
11. HTTPS/security-related delivery issues that affect search access
12. Internationalization/hreflang when applicable

## Method
- Prefer observed evidence from crawl/source/headers/tools.
- Distinguish confirmed defects from suspected risks.
- Prioritize by impact, affected URL count, confidence, and implementation effort.
- Re-test after changes.

## Completion gate
Do not call an audit complete without stating what was actually tested, what could not be tested, and remaining risks.
